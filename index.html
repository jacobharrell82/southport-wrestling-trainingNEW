Training Philosophy

We are teaching athletes how to train—not simply how to lift heavy weights.

  • Movement quality first
  • Explosiveness without sacrificing control
  • Progress at the athlete's level
  • Consistency over maxes

At-Home Athletic Workout

You can improve athleticism without a weight room. Perform 3 rounds, resting as needed.

10 Bodyweight Squats
8 Push-Ups
8/leg Reverse Lunges
10 Hip Hinges
5 Broad Jumps
20 sec Plank
20 yd Farmer Carry if safe

Everything should look good before you make it harder.

Weight Room Rules

Technique comes before weight. If technique breaks down, stop the set or reduce the weight.
  • Ask a coach when you are unsure.
  • Never chase another athlete's weight.
  • Olympic lifts are not conditioning exercises.
  • Keep your lifting area organized.
  • Use equipment only as instructed.
  • At home, use only safe equipment and spaces.
